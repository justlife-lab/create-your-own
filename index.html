FAQs

Before you book

Everything you need to know about building your own lab test at home.

What is a create-your-own lab test?

Instead of buying a fixed package, you choose individual tests (parameters) such as Vitamin D, HbA1c or TSH. You only pay for the tests you add.

Do I need to fast?

Some tests, like fasting blood sugar, lipid profile and insulin, need 8–12 hours of fasting. Water is fine. These tests carry a "Fasting" tag, and we remind you before you book. Morning slots work best for fasting tests.

Is there a minimum order?

No. You can book a single test. Home sample collection is included in the price shown.

What times can the nurse come?

Every day from 6:00 AM to 11:30 PM in 1-hour windows. Same-day slots close 1 hour before they start.

How do I get my results?

Results are shared digitally once the lab releases them, usually within 24–48 hours. Some specialised tests can take longer.

Can I change my slot?

Yes. Our team calls to confirm your booking and can move it to another available slot.

How do I pay?

You pay on the day of the visit. The total you see when you book is the total you pay.

How is my data used?

We use your details only to arrange the home visit and share your results. Your health data is handled in line with UAE health data regulations.
